## Review finding and fix

Normal asynchronous classification could finish after a newer human task and overwrite that newer task's session pin. Earlier protection covered stall escalation only. The fix reserves a bounded, expiring session slot before awaiting classification and commits only if that slot still owns the session. Pending slots cannot serve as pins or trigger escalation. A controlled deferred-response regression checks that the newer task keeps its pin after the old response arrives.

The full suite, type checks and focused tests passed. Affinity and escalation remain disabled in the experimental fragment, but the concurrency fix protects future opt-in use. No production deployment or activation was performed.

Remaining gates before activation: realistic coding/tool outcomes; live quota/load shadow behavior; long-context and multimodal capability validation; provider timeout/access follow-ups. The successful access and selector tests are necessary but insufficient for rollout.
